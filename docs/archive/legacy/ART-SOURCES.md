## Animation update (current gameplay)

The built-in imagegen tool produced three transparent animation atlases from the approved character designs: `../assets/source/atlases/heroes-animation.png` (4×4), `../assets/runtime/characters/enemies/shared/legion-animation.png` (4×6), `../assets/runtime/characters/enemies/shared/centurion-animation.png` (4×2). There are 48 poses total: four walking and four attack poses for Asterix, Obelix, three legionaries and the centurion. These supersede the earlier bobbing-only rendering. Earlier sections below describe the original still assets, retained for portraits and load-error fallback.

`../data/animations/animation-layout.js` records measured opaque bounding boxes so fists, spearheads and helmets extending over an implicit grid edge are retained. `../src/rendering/art/animation-art.js` prepares cached frames, aligns feet and normalizes facing direction. Attack damage/release triggers at 50% of action duration (third frame), once per action; recovery ends at 100%. Walking phase advances with distance travelled. During pause and upgrades, game animation clocks stop. Legionaries telegraph a thrust, stop, and can miss if the player leaves reach before impact.

Animation preview with state selection, speed, pause and frame stepping: `../previews/animation-preview.html`.

# Отдельные ресурсы Астерикса и Обеликса

Каждый герой загружает собственные `portrait.png` и `animations.png` из `assets/runtime/characters/heroes/asterix/` или `assets/runtime/characters/heroes/obelix/`. В каждом анимационном файле четыре кадра движения и четыре кадра атаки. Координаты хранятся под отдельными ключами `asterix` и `obelix` в `data/animations/animation-layout.json`.

Файлы извлечены из исходных атласов без масштабирования и изменения рисунков. Общие оригиналы сохранены в `assets/source/atlases/`; повторное извлечение: `powershell -File tools/split-hero-assets.ps1`. После изменения ресурсов или координат выполните `npm run generate`. Размеры героев и положение ног при отрисовке сохранены.
