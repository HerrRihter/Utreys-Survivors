Audio is bundled locally; the game does not contact the source sites.

- `music/battle.mp3`: **Battle Theme A**, cynicmusic (https://cynicmusic.com / https://pixelsphere.org). Battle loop. https://opengameart.org/content/battle-theme-a

  Original: https://opengameart.org/sites/default/files/defeat_0.mp3

Music files renamed without editing. Looping is handled by the player; the full tracks may have a transition at the repeat point.

## Enemy defeat voices and fist swing

`death-1.ogg`, `death-2.ogg`, `death-3.ogg`: **Pain sounds by EmoPreben**,
Lasse Bührmann (EmoPreben), CC0 1.0.
https://opengameart.org/content/pain-sounds-by-emopreben
Archive: https://opengameart.org/sites/default/files/painsounds.zip
Original files: `Painsounds v2 - Track 1 - Arrrch.ogg`,
`Painsounds v2 - Track 2 - Hurgh.ogg`, `Painsounds v2 - Track 5 - Urggh.ogg`.
Downloaded 2026-10-11; renamed without editing.

`punch-swing.wav` (unused legacy asset): original procedural air/cloth swish generated by
`tools/generate-punch-swing.cjs`. No third-party samples. 0.16 seconds,
44.1 kHz mono PCM; rendered offline, not synthesized during combat.

## User replacements

The active effects now use `punch.mp3`, `hit-1.mp3`, `hit-2.mp3` and
`hurt.mp3`, supplied in the workspace by the user. Source/license metadata
was not supplied for these replacements. The matching legacy OGG/WAV files
above remain in the source folder but are not included in the published build.
